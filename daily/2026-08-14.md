- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09096

### HydroNet — data-guided FVM-PINN for 2D SWE

- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11001
- Code: https://github.com/psu-efd/HydroNet
- Type: Paper + open MIT tooling
- Why it matters: strong-form residual PINN이 SWE에 부적합(국소 보존·불연속·비정렬 메시) → **well-balanced Roe FVM residual on unstructured mesh**. 핵심 결과: **physics-only FVM-PINN은 현실 2D에서 collapse** 가능 → data guidance + λ-mixed FV residual 필요.
- Evidence: cs.LG 2026-05-09; physics-only L2(|u|) **0.166**, L2(h) **0.075** → data-guided mixes down to L2(|u|) **~0.032** (Issue Board ablation harvest). GitHub MIT ★9, forks 3, Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20099995.
- Caveat: 2D SWE focus; metropolitan flood digital-twin scale는 별 계열.
- VA relevance: Urban_Flighter / SWE conservation 게이트; residual-only green-light 거절 언어 + FV flux 계약.
- Action: Read/Test/Save
