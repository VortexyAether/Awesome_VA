## New Papers / Tools

### TIDE — 3D incompressible DNS ensemble benchmark for SciML

- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04222
- Code: https://github.com/Dyloong1/TIDE-dataset-benchmark
- Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/ydai17/TIDE
- Type: Paper + open benchmark / DNS corpus
- Why it matters: Most turbulence-ML work stays 2D or ships one realization per config. TIDE is a **256³ DNS corpus** with **15 configurations × independent ensembles**, pressure fields, equation-level residual checks, five tasks, audited baselines, and physical-fidelity metrics beside pointwise error.
- Evidence: README/paper — ~2.6 TB, 134 trajectories, CC-BY-4.0 data + MIT code (`Dyloong1/TIDE-dataset-benchmark` ★1, pushed 2026-08-06). Main forecasting configs: learned models **barely beat persistence** and still make about **twice the error of a spectral solver given the true equations**; low pointwise error can still distort small-scale dynamics; forced→decay transfer exposes missing conditioning on external drive.
- Caveat: Periodic-box spectral DNS substrate, not wall-bounded product CFD meshes; corpus size is large (~TB). Early stars — treat as benchmark infrastructure, not a drop-in Urban_Flighter trainer overnight.
- Action: Read/Cite/Save/Test

### Multi-fidelity neural operators under non-trivial fidelity gaps

- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04708
- Type: Paper / multi-fidelity neural-operator benchmark study
- Why it matters: Multi-fidelity NO papers often only change grid resolution. This study adds cases where LF/HF **governing equations or temporal dynamics differ**, closer to RANS↔LES-style industrial mismatch, and ranks two-step / residual / intermediate / transfer strategies.
- Evidence: Transfer learning (LF weights → HF init) **consistently beats HF-only** across the suite; methods that feed LF predictions directly into HF models amplify error under large LF–HF discrepancy, especially autoregressive smoke-inflow.
- Caveat: No public monorepo harvested; “smoke inflow” and modified Darcy are still controlled PDEs, not full industrial CFD stacks.
- Action: Read/Cite

### Hybrid-Joint — differentiable neural-CFD joint SGS + wall closures

- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17357
- Type: Paper / differentiable hybrid LES closure learning
- Why it matters: WMLES usually trains SGS, wall model, and numerics as if independent. Hybrid-Joint learns **composed neural operators + fixed structure layers** for SGS and wall **jointly inside a differentiable flow solver**, using only low-order statistics.
- Evidence: ZPG TBL a posteriori tests **Re_θ ≈ 600–6500**; extrapolates beyond 4× highest training Re; recovers log-law mean velocity without imposing it in the wall closure; spectral energy recovered though spectra are not in the training objective. Ablations: learning either closure alone is insufficient.
- Caveat: Paper-only at harvest (no first-party code). Still wall-bounded channel/TBL class — transfer to complex geometry needs separate gates.
- Action: Read/Cite

### Multi-granularity conformal prediction for automotive aero neural operators

- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17297
- Code: https://github.com/DevinJia19/Multi-Granularity-Conformal-Prediction-for-Reliable-Neural-Operator-Automotive-Aerodynamic-Surrogate
- Type: Paper + early open reliability layer for aero surrogates
- Why it matters: Deterministic NO drag/Cp/WSS maps do not say **which vehicle or surface patch** needs CFD follow-up. Adds conformalized intervals at case-level (Cd) and spatially adaptive residual-normalized intervals for surface fields on DrivAerML (GeoTransolver / Transolver).
- Evidence: 90% nominal coverage target; OOF aggregation cuts Monte Carlo coverage std **10.41 → 3.10 pp**; point-adaptive normalized calibration narrows mean interval width **~22.7% (pressure)** and **~25–27% (WSS)** vs coarser schemes. Repo exists (★1, license null via API).
- Caveat: Early repo / license unclear; conformal coverage is finite-sample and split-dependent — still needs CFD verification budget, not a free pass.
- Action: Read/Cite/Watch

### Ortho2CAD — orthographic drawings → editable CadQuery

- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08891
- Code: https://github.com/AdityaJoglekar/Ortho2CAD
- Type: Paper + open VLM CAD reconstruction
- Why it matters: Engineering intent often lives in **raster orthographic drawings**, while CAE needs parametric editable 3D. Ortho2CAD maps drawings → CadQuery via SFT + geometry-grounded RL, with a pythonOCC drawing generator (hidden lines + dimensions).
- Evidence: Claimed **100% syntactically valid** code on reported settings; average **>7% relative IoU gain** vs next-best baseline. GitHub `AdityaJoglekar/Ortho2CAD` ★7 (license null via API at fetch).
- Caveat: Drawing→CadQuery IoU ≠ manufacturing/printability/BRep feature-tree/sim-ready watertight; license still needs local check before product use.
- Action: Read/Test/Save
