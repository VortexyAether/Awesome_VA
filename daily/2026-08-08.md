### Newton–Krylov correction of steady CFD surrogates (OOD airfoils)

- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04400
- Type: Paper / solver-coupled surrogate deploy pattern
- Why it matters: Pure neural field prediction fails under OOD geometry. This work uses the surrogate as a **warm-start**, then finishes with **Newton–Krylov** so industrial workflows keep classical residual convergence at the terminal stage.
- Evidence: Abstract — OOD bench from real **transonic airfoil optimization trajectories**; median residual \(L_2\) ratio improved by **over seven orders of magnitude** with lower field/aero error; supercritical airfoil optimization claims **15.5×** generation-level speedup vs CFD; 3D flying-wing extension tested.
- Caveat: No public code harvested; steady CFD scope (not full unsteady product CFD). HTML table shell was weak at Issue Board harvest — treat exact residual tables as abstract-level until PDF-verified.
- Action: Read/Cite
